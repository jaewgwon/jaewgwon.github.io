Support Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TIE: Today I Earned (“TIE”, “the app”, “we”) handles your information. The short version: TIE does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Data we collect

None. TIE has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect any personal or usage data.

Information you enter

The settings you provide — such as your monthly net salary, work hours, currency, preferred language, theme, and holiday region — are stored only on your own Mac, using the operating system's standard local storage (UserDefaults). This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Removing the app removes this data.

Network access

TIE works fully offline. It makes a network request in only one case: when you select a region under Public Holidays, the app fetches that region's public holiday dates from the third-party service Nager.Date (date.nager.at).

  • This request contains only a calendar year and a country code (for example, US, JP, or KR).
  • It contains no salary information, no personal data, and no device or user identifiers.
  • The result is cached locally so the app keeps working offline.
  • If you set Public Holidays to None, TIE makes no network requests at all.

Your use of the Nager.Date service is subject to its own terms; we receive no data back about you from it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

TIE does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at jaewgwon@gmail.com.
