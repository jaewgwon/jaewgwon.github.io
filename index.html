Frequently Asked Questions

How does TIE calculate my earnings?

You enter your monthly net salary and your daily work hours once. From the moment your workday starts, TIE divides your pay across your working time and counts up second by second. Weekends and public holidays are excluded automatically.

Where is my salary information stored?

Everything you enter stays on your Mac. TIE does not have an account system and never uploads your salary or settings anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does TIE need an internet connection?

No. TIE works fully offline. The only optional network request is to fetch public holiday dates for the region you choose; that request contains only a year and a country code, never any personal data. Set Public Holidays to None to disable it entirely.

How do I hide my earnings from others?

Click the menu bar item and choose Hide Amount to replace the number with “TIE”. You can also enable Hide Earnings in Screen Capture in Settings so your earnings never appear in screenshots or screen recordings.

The amount doesn't move / seems wrong.

Open Settings and confirm your monthly net salary, work start/end times, currency, and public-holiday region are correct. Earnings only accrue during your configured work hours on working days.

How do I make TIE start automatically?

Enable Launch at Login in Settings.
